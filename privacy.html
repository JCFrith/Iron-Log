Iron Log

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

The short version

Iron Log stores your account details, training log, food log and any health records you upload, so they follow you between devices, and shares them with your coach so they can coach you. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we don't use analytics or trackers. You can export your training log and have everything deleted.

What we collect

  • Account information: email address, name, and a hashed password (we never see or store your password in readable form).
  • Training data: workouts you log (exercises, weights, reps, effort ratings, session and exercise notes, deload history, activity sessions) and your assigned training program.
  • Body metrics: bodyweight and measurements, if you log them.
  • Food log: foods, portions, meals, recipes and custom foods you save, and your calorie and macro targets.
  • Health records (optional): body-composition scans (such as DEXA) and lab results you enter or upload, including the photo or PDF file itself. Files are kept in private storage that only you and your coach can open.
  • Camera (optional): used only while you scan a barcode or photograph a nutrition label or record. Barcode scanning happens on your device. A photo is stored only if you save it as a record.
  • Preferences: settings such as your effort scale.
  • WHOOP data (optional): if you connect a WHOOP account, we receive the data you authorize (such as recovery, sleep, strain and profile information) to display it in the app and inform your training. We never receive your WHOOP password. You can disconnect WHOOP at any time, which stops all further access.
  • Feedback: anything you send through the in-app feedback form.

We do not collect location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or anything from your device beyond what's listed above.

How your data is used

  • To run the app: sync your history across devices, calculate training and nutrition targets, and show your progress.
  • To enable coaching: your coach on Iron Log can view your training log, notes, body metrics, food log, health records and (if connected) recovery data. Your coach can also set your program and nutrition targets and leave notes on your sessions. This visibility is the point of the product; if you do not want a coach to see this data, do not log it in a coached account.
  • To respond if you contact us for support.

We do not sell or rent your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising of any kind.

Other services the app talks to

  • Supabase: our database, file storage and sign-in provider. All of your stored data lives here.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts: when you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode is sent to these public food databases to look it up. Your account details are not sent, but they can see the request and your IP address, as any website can.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the barcode-scanning code to your browser the first time you open the scanner.
  • WHOOP: only if you connect it.
  • GitHub Pages and Vercel: host the app's files.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase and transmitted over HTTPS. Access is enforced with row-level security: you can access your own records, and coach accounts can additionally read their athletes' records. A copy of your training log is also kept on your own device so the app works with a weak gym signal. Signing out removes the sign-in from the device; the local copy of the log stays until you clear it or the browser's site data.

Your controls

  • Export: download your training log and body metrics as JSON anytime from the System panel. For a copy of your food log or health records, email us.
  • Delete a record: deleting a scan or lab record in the app also deletes its uploaded file.
  • Delete everything: email us at jcfrith@gmail.com from your account email and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data, including uploaded files, within 30 days.
  • Disconnect WHOOP: revoke Iron Log's access anytime from the app or from your WHOOP account settings.
  • Local-only mode: you can use the training log without an account; that data then stays on your device and is never uploaded. The food log and health records need an account.

Health disclaimer

Iron Log records fitness data and displays training targets. It is not a medical device and provides no medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before beginning or changing an exercise program.

Children

Iron Log is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the effective date above and note the change in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
